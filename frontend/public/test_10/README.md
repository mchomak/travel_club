# TEST_10 — photographic selection

Five new images were researched on primary Pexels photo pages on 17 October 2026. Sources, photographers, original download URLs, dimensions and local file sizes are recorded in `manifest.json`. The [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) allows website use and modification; do not imply the endorsement of a person shown in an image.

The images are **illustrative stock photographs**. They are not photographs of Travel Club friends, clients, or a confirmed future itinerary. This distinction is also visible in the spreads. Locations reported by photographers are provenance, not additional claims about the Thailand trip. In particular, no specific Thai island or city neighbourhood is attributed to the real trip. The NEXT ISSUE image depicts Guilin and does not announce a destination.

The selection progresses from green karst landscape, through a quiet misty river, to Thai coastline, life beside the sea, and Bangkok street texture. Images are locally optimized WebP (quality 83, maximum 1800 × 2200 pixels), with controlled CSS crops. No remote image dependency remains at runtime. No generative image assets are used.

Brand signature SVGs are reused from `/test_9/TC_Logo_Editorial_Signature_Cream.svg` and `/test_9/TC_Logo_Editorial_Signature_Navy.svg`; they are project assets, separate from this photo license.
