`deformation.ts` samples a 2.32 × 2.9 leaf by integrating tangent directions at fixed arc-length increments. The tangent combines smoothstep flip progress, an evolving curl front, a stiffness coefficient, lift, and a small height-dependent corner lead. Every midsurface spine point stays at x=z=0; row arc length stays constant; endpoints are flat. The height perturbation changes the outer edge without fabric-like waves. Defaults are bend2.05/curl0.9/stiffness0.24/lift0.35, with independently tested internal spineConstraint0.12 and outerEdgeInfluence0.15. Curl forms smoothly up to .4, peaks .4-.6, and releases with zero envelope velocity at touchdown. The controller adds two surfaces offset along the local normal, reversed backside UVs, and closed edges; 56 × 16 segments on desktop, 32 × 16 on compact initial viewports. Only the active leaf deforms. The cover has 0.97 stiffness and attenuated bend/curl.

`timeline.ts` deterministically samples initial hold, 0.64s cover opening, three 0.68s paper turns, and 0.14s spread holds. Default total is 3.46s. Duration controls preserve normalized sequence position and the current pause state. Seeking pauses; Replay resets the entire clock. Reduced motion starts/returns at China; dynamic preference changes stop motion. Hidden documents suspend the clock without catch-up. A final state has no ongoing RAF; controls and resize render one new frame.
